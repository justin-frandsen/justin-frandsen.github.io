## The Role of Statistical Learning in Attentional Guidance During Search Through Naturalistic Scenes

This poster was presented at **VSS 2024**, exploring how statistical regularities in natural scenes guide visual attention. The project examines how learned object–location associations shape attentional priorities during search, connecting experience-driven learning and naturalistic perception.
