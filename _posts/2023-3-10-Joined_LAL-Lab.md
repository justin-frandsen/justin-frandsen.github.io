I joined the **Learning and Attention Lab** at **Texas A&M University**, working under [**Dr. Brian A. Anderson**](https://artsci.tamu.edu/psychological-brain-sciences/contact/profiles/brian-anderson.html).  

Our research examines how attention adapts through experience — a perfect fit for my interests in visual learning and reward-based attention.  
I’m thrilled to be part of such a dynamic and innovative lab community.
