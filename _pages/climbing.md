Rock climbing is one of my favorite hobbies. I love the physical and mental challenge it offers. Sending a problem requires problem solving and strong physical ability. It helps me stay healthy, de-stress, and spend time with friends both indoors and outdoors.

I climb primarily in two styles: **bouldering** and **lead climbing**.  
- **Bouldering** involves climbing short, powerful sequences over pads.  
- **Lead climbing** involves clipping into bolts with a rope as you ascend a taller wall or cliff.

Indoors, I love training on the **Tension Board**, where I’m currently working toward sending **V9**. Outside, I enjoy exploring new boulders and routes around the country whenever I have the chance.

You can follow my climbing and outdoor progress on Instagram:  
[@just_in_climbs](https://www.instagram.com/just_in_climbs)

Yes, **“just_in_climbs”** is a play on *“just in time.”*
